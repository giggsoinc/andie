# Privacy Policy — Andie

**Last updated: May 12, 2026**
**Published by: Giggso Inc**

---

## What Andie Collects

Andie is a local Claude Code skill. It does not collect, transmit, or store any data on Giggso-operated servers.

All conversations and outputs stay within your Claude Code session. No data is sent to Giggso.

---

## Third-Party Services

Andie operates entirely within Claude Code's context. It makes no external API calls of its own.

If Andie routes to a specialist skill that calls an external service (e.g. a domain-specific tool), that service's own privacy policy applies.

---

## What Andie Does Not Do

- Does not collect telemetry or usage data
- Does not phone home to Giggso servers
- Does not transmit your conversations or prompts externally
- Does not store session data beyond your local Claude Code session
