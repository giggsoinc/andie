## How to activate

Once installed on any platform:

| Say this | What happens |
|---|---|
| `deep` | Expert explains anything — whiteboard clarity, Feynman rules, 3 levels |
| `drama` | Expert panel debates your decision — 3 levels, documents gated |
| `movie` | Same as drama |
| `triage` | War strategy applied to your crisis — dynamic strategy, battle plan |
| `war zone` | Same as triage |
| `kaizen` | Structured improvement — 7 wastes, one fix at a time |
| `factory` | Same as kaizen |

**On every mode:** Andie first reads your prompt, asks up to 5 targeted questions (with prefixed A/B/C options), then enters the mode. Documents are never auto-generated — Andie shows findings first and asks for confirmation before writing each one.

---

## What you get

**Deep** delivers:
- Expert-level explanation at 3 depths (5yr / engineer / expert)
- One analogy per concept — whiteboard style, no jargon
- Devil's Advocate challenge after every expert explanation
- Domain insight — what the expert would do next

**Drama** delivers:
- Expert panel debate across 3 levels (Position → Challenge → Synthesis)
- Findings summary + confirmation gate before each document
- Strategy document · ADR · Action plan · Executive summary

**Triage** delivers:
- Dynamic war strategy selection (OODA, Blitzkrieg, Schwerpunkt, etc.)
- 6-person war room panel with hands-on practitioners
- Battle plan with 24h actions, go/no-go decision, OODA mapping

**Kaizen** delivers:
- 7-waste scan (Lean) of your system or process
- DMAIC structured improvement — one waste at a time
- Control mechanism to prevent regression

**All modes:**
- Understand Phase — up to 5 targeted questions before engaging
- Pre-Document Gate — findings summary + Y/N/Edit before every document
- Dynamic names — multi-ethnic, multi-religious practitioners every session
- 4 dimensions: Strategic · Operational · Tactical · Logistical
